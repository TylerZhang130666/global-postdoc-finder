# Global postdoc source guide

Verify every opportunity on its authoritative page and record the access date. Search three channels separately: advertised jobs, candidate/host-applied fellowships, and host discovery. Never describe a fellowship as an existing job.

### China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan

- Mainland China national postdoctoral system/reference: https://www.chinapostdoctor.org.cn/
- Mainland China: search official university and Chinese Academy of Sciences institute sites.
- Hong Kong: search official HKU, CUHK, HKUST, CityU, PolyU, and other institution career pages.
- Taiwan: search Academia Sinica and university career pages: https://www.sinica.edu.tw/en

For mainland China search `博士后招聘` plus discipline and institution. Treat registration systems and job advertisements separately. Verify housing, municipal subsidies, performance conditions, and entry/exit procedures.

### India and wider South/Southeast Asia

Search official pages for IITs, IISc, IISERs, CSIR institutes, TIFR, national laboratories, universities, and funding agencies. Elsewhere prioritize official university, national research agency, and government institute pages.

## Verification rules

- **Primary:** employer, funder, project, government, or official national/regional portal.
- **Secondary:** reputable aggregator linking to a primary source.
- **Lead only:** social post, mailing list, repost, or search snippet.
- `open`: authoritative page has a future deadline or explicitly says open.
- `rolling`: authoritative page explicitly states rolling review.
- `uncertain`: missing/conflicting date, inaccessible details, or unclear status.
- `closed`: deadline passed or page says closed/filled.
- `monitor`: group, institute, funding call, or portal rather than a vacancy.

Preserve currency, pay period, gross/net status, contract duration, employee/fellow status, and exact source wording. State sponsorship facts only when explicitly supported.
